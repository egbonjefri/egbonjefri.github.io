Jeffery Osagie

I'm a physiologist and software developer in Winnipeg.

My MSc at the University of Manitoba was on a 3D-bioprinted airway smooth muscle model for studying asthma. Part of that work meant photographing bioprinted airways before and after adding contractile agents, then manually measuring how much each airway narrowed. I got into programming trying to automate that tedious work. I never went back.

These days I write mostly Java, Python, R and JavaScript/TypeScript. I'm currently designing a small processor using only NOR gates, with a longer-term idea in mind: building computers out of proteins.

I also like logic and philosophy, chess, and puzzle games like Portal and The Talos Principle.
